Grove Mail

A private, personal mail helper. It is not a public service.

What it is

Grove Mail is a small tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It lets the owner's personal assistant software read and organize the owner's own Gmail accounts, so that mail from a few people who matter (collaborators, care providers, school) is noticed and filed on time.

What it does

  • Searches and reads messages in the owner's own Gmail accounts.
  • Applies or removes labels, for example to mark a message as sorted or to archive it.
  • Reads files in the owner's own Google Drive, read-only, when the owner asks.

What it does not do

  • It does not send, forward or delete email.
  • It does not change or delete Drive files.
  • It is not offered to anyone else. Only the owner's own Google accounts use it.
